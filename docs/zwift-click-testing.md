# Testing the Zwift Click with VeloForge

Thank you for trying this! VeloForge now has virtual shifting for a
single-cog bike (like the Zwift Cog), and can use a Zwift Click as the
shifter. Nobody on our side has a Click, so your test is the first with
a real one. It takes about 10 minutes.

## Before you start

- Close **Zwift** and the **Zwift Companion** app on every device (phone
  and computer). A Click talks to one app at a time.
- Have your trainer and the Click nearby. Press a Click button to wake it.

## 1. Turn on virtual shifting

1. Open **Devices**.
2. Tick **Virtual shifting (for a single-cog setup like Zwift Cog)**.
3. Connect your trainer as usual if it isn't already.

## 2. Pair the Click

1. Press a button on the Click so it wakes (its light blinks).
2. In **Devices**, press **Scan for devices**.
3. The Click appears as **Zwift Click** with the label *Shift buttons
   (Zwift)*. A **Click v2** appears twice, as *right half (+)* and
   *left half (−)*: connect both.
4. Press **Connect**. Under **Connections** its card should say
   *Press + or − to try it*.

## 3. Check the gear changes

1. Press **+** a few times, then **−** a few times.
2. On the Click's card in **Devices**, the press count and *last: +* or
   *last: −* should change with each press.
3. The gear under **Virtual shifting** (e.g. *50x21 · gear 10/15*) should
   go up with **+** and down with **−**, lighting up orange for a moment.
4. Go to **Ride** and start a **Free ride**. Pedal steadily and shift:
   the gear shows at the top right of the 3D view, and pedalling should
   get harder with **+** and easier with **−**, coming on smoothly over
   about a second.

On a **Click v2**, the **−** half may not respond. Zwift locks it until
the Zwift app unlocks it. If so, try the **B** button on the right half,
which also shifts down.

## 4. Send us the log

Whether it worked or not:

1. Go to **Devices**.
2. Press **Copy log** (next to *Log*, on the right).
3. Paste it into your reply, and tell us:
   - Click v1 or Click v2, and which buttons worked;
   - whether the gear shown changed, and whether the pedalling got harder
     and easier;
   - your computer (Linux, Windows or Mac) and your trainer.

If something went wrong, also tick **Log raw sensor data**, press each
Click button a few times, then **Copy log** again: that records every
message the Click sends.
